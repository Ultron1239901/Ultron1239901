I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at **Dr. Ambedkar Institute of Technology, Bengaluru** (CGPA 9.17), building at the intersection of **data analytics, applied machine learning, and full-stack product engineering**. My work centers on turning messy, real-world datasets into dashboards, models, and systems that drive actual business decisions — not just notebooks that live in isolation.

On the analytics side, I work fluently across **Power BI, Excel, Google Sheets, SQL, and the Python data stack (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn)** to move from raw data to insight. On the engineering side, I design and ship **FastAPI/Flask backends, React frontends, and cloud-orchestrated infrastructure on AWS**, with a growing focus on multi-agent AI systems for automation.

I approach every project with a **product mindset**: identify the real decision a stakeholder needs to make, build the shortest reliable path to that decision, and instrument it so it keeps working after I walk away.

**🎯 Open To:** Data Analyst roles · AI/ML Engineering internships · Full-stack collaboration · Open-source contribution
